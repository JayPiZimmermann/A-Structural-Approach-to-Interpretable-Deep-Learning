# Academic reviews of the master's thesis

The thesis has the research substance for an excellent 1.0 assessment: a meaningful unifying idea, substantial mathematics, extensive experiments, and unusually serious proof and artifact infrastructure. The present manuscript nevertheless contains several checkable reporting errors, a statistical sampling problem, and incomplete reproduction instructions. Those defects prevent an unqualified recommendation of the highest grade in its current form. The most valuable next work is a focused correction and evidence audit of the existing contribution.

These twelve agent-produced academic reviews assess the thesis from distinct perspectives. They are candid advisory assessments, not reports from appointed examiners or an official grade. The [assessment framework](assessment-criteria.md) was established before the reviews. It explains the expectations for excellent independent scientific work, distinguishes them from conference acceptance criteria, and gives experimental rigor and reproducibility substantial weight. It does not invent numerical grading weights or demand unlimited research breadth.

The publication basis is now explicit and should remain so: **Hidden Monotonicity** is a coauthored **CVPR 2026 Highlight paper**, and **Playing the Network Backward** is a coauthored **arXiv preprint, version 2**; the student is first author of both. The [official CVPR listing](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2026/poster/36564) and [versioned arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06212v2) support this distinction. The Highlight is a significant research achievement. The preprint should be assessed on its content, and the integrated thesis must explain the student's contribution within both collaborations.

## The overall academic judgment

The shared argument is substantive: one construction exposes a structured representation, while the other makes backward attribution an explicit mathematical object. The thesis connects them through network decompositions instead of merely placing two papers together. The revised game campaign also has real methodological strengths: separate validation and test data, objective-specific configuration selection, paired comparisons, per-image scores, disclosed negative trade-offs, and transfer with frozen game settings. The formalization is explicitly partial and distinguishes exact mathematics from Python implementation and experimental evidence.

A fresh CPU run reconstructed **all 13 supported table outputs** from the shipped game summaries, with canonical matches and exit status 0. This is useful, executed evidence. It establishes reconstruction of those tables through the official writer and thesis adapter; it does not establish a new model-evaluation run or coverage of the missing diagnostics and older split-study inputs. See the [run log](../evidence/tex-resumption/table-reproduction.log) and [exit status](../evidence/tex-resumption/table-reproduction.exit).

The strongest criticism is that the manuscript's broad summaries are less careful than its best technical sections. Some claims exceed the tables, some interpretations exceed the measured quantities, and some documented commands cannot execute as written. These are scientific communication and validity problems. Publication prestige, more experiments of the same kind, or a larger Lean theorem count would not settle them. Conversely, correcting them does not require a third paper, complete formalization, or a repeat of the entire expensive evaluation campaign.

## Corrections to prioritize before submission

The following order places correctness and evidence interpretation before optional expansion. A correction can be complete through narrower wording where the existing evidence supports that narrower claim. A documented limitation is appropriate when evidence cannot be recovered; it must then constrain the conclusion.

1. **Correct the false stream-to-logit implication and complete mathematical scope.** For the defined DICIN, the full derivative is the sum of the two stream derivatives. A positive complemented-stream derivative alone need not imply that adding an empty pixel increases the class logit; the interpretability review supplies a counterexample within the stated architecture. Describe stream-local sensitivity, complete-model local sensitivity, and finite counterfactual change separately. Also state the signed affine-head bridge, define attention normalization at zero mass, and attach convexity/monotonicity guarantees to the variants that actually satisfy them. **Done:** definitions, captions, and headline claims agree with the precise constructions, and the identified boundary examples have defined outcomes. See [interpretability](aspect-interpretability-validity.md) and [mathematics](aspect-mathematical-rigor.md).

2. **Reconcile empirical claims with every eligible reported result.** The full ResNet appendix reports Guided Grad-CAM AL **0.742**, above the quoted SplitCAM **0.673**. For Selectivity, Guided Backprop **3.517** is the relevant best baseline against SplitCAM **3.503**; the observed gap is **0.014**, not the **0.187** obtained by choosing LayerCAM. The abstract's cross-category claim is also too broad: ResNet Maximum Sensitivity favors Grad-CAM **0.131** over the best displayed split value **0.219**, with lower better. Correct winner and ablation summaries, and explain repeated configurations with differing values rather than silently changing cells. **Done:** a compact claim ledger identifies campaign, metric direction, eligible rows, configuration, effect size, uncertainty status, and source artifact. See [results and claims](aspect-results-claims.md).

3. **Repair VOC uncertainty using the actual independent unit.** The released scores contain **2,743 image–class records from 2,293 images**; 375 images contribute multiple records. The current aggregation resamples records individually. Define the intended image or image–class estimand, resample images as clusters while preserving within-image records and method pairing, and use compatible inference for binary outcomes. **Done:** regenerate intervals and comparison decisions from the existing scores and report which conclusions change. This finding concerns uncertainty justification; it does not establish that the reported means are wrong or that the main gains disappear. See [statistics](aspect-statistics.md).

4. **Make the written evaluation specification reproduce the implemented population and metric.** Document the game's **752 masks → 616 label-agreeing images → 50 validation and 566 test images → 565 crop-visible test images**. Separate spatial pixels from channel-wise input features in the Pixel Flipping definition so its stated settings yield 96 steps. State each campaign's explained class policy and treatment of misclassified images. **Done:** the manuscript, split identifiers, loader, and metric equations agree; the older campaign's provenance is established separately. See [experimental design](aspect-experimental-design.md) and [baseline fairness](aspect-baseline-fairness.md).

5. **Narrow statistical and semantic interpretations.** Replace nonsignificance-as-comparability with the actual test conclusion, and near-zero Spearman correlation-as-independence with weak observed rank association. State the differing ViT randomization interventions beside their interpretation. Qualify test-selected-best significance shading until its inferential family is justified. Cross-dataset transfer establishes portability of the selected settings, while broader heatmaps illustrate context attribution; neither alone identifies model-specific mechanisms or perceptual optimality. **Done:** titles, captions, summaries, and conclusions use the same evidence-bounded language. Stronger analyses are needed only if stronger claims are retained. See [statistics](aspect-statistics.md) and [interpretability](aspect-interpretability-validity.md).

6. **Repair executable instructions and availability guarantees.** Correct the Hidden Monotonicity flag and example mismatches, and release the missing multi-seed configurations or explicitly mark that training route incomplete. Reconcile the game guide's “every result table” promise with its unavailable diagnostics, and reserve bit-exact claims for demonstrated conditions. **Done:** every advertised entry point passes a parser/import smoke check; a small named example runs; the artifact inventory gives an input location and command or a truthful unavailable status for each central result. Preserve pinned upstream provenance through versioned corrections or explicit companion instructions. See [artifact execution](aspect-artifact-execution.md) and [reproducibility](aspect-reproducibility.md).

7. **Correct the cost account and identify measurement conditions.** The displayed SplitCAM times are approximately **14–29 times** those of ordinary Grad-CAM/LayerCAM in the corresponding comparisons. Name the methods with which cost is actually competitive, correct the forward/backward description of alpha, and describe potential optimization without calling measured overhead a proved upper bound. **Done:** central timings identify implementation revision, precision, hardware, timing scope, variability, and failures; re-time only unresolved central comparisons. See [numerical stability](aspect-numerical-stability.md).

8. **Make the declaration and disclosure factually consistent.** Supply the actual tool products, providers, versions or retained identifiers, purposes, and affected activities promised by the declaration. Distinguish original paper preparation from subsequent thesis and Lean work; record unknown historical identifiers accurately. **Done:** the author can truthfully sign the declaration against the disclosed information. This is a documentation inconsistency, not an inference of misconduct. See [authorship and provenance](aspect-authorship-provenance.md).

## Work needed for a more persuasive 1.0 case

After those corrections, five bounded improvements would strengthen the examination argument substantially:

- **Demonstrate a credible empirical reproduction route.** Recover the central older split-study score/configuration artifacts and one important trajectory diagnostic where possible. Pin or reconstruct the older metric environment, including a small Pixel Flipping fixture. Capture a clean, bounded run linking inputs, checkpoints, configurations, outputs, and tolerances. Distinguish installation smoke tests, reconstruction from saved scores, and actual reevaluation.
- **Validate numerical stabilization on its own terms.** For a fixed backward rule, report pre- and post-assimilation residuals, correction magnitudes, precision effects, nonfinite values, and gate disagreements on a small synthetic sweep and a specified real-image subset. The alpha sweep changes the attribution rule and cannot alone establish reduced roundoff error.
- **Explain selection and comparison scope.** Report a validation-only stability analysis from existing scores, or explicitly limit parameter claims to the realized split. Justify fixed CNN CAM layers and unequal grids. A small matched check is useful where a headline comparison depends on those choices; exhaustive equal-size searches are unnecessary.
- **Make independence and synthesis visible.** Add an author-confirmed contribution/provenance table and a research-question-to-evidence table. Identify which results are inherited, extended, corrected, or newly synthesized, and answer each question with its supporting theorem or experiment and remaining limit. Author roles cannot be inferred from first authorship alone.
- **Expose proof certification boundaries.** Preserve numbered Lean links while adding an examiner-facing scope table and a few worked TeX-to-Lean correspondence explanations. Bind the final PDF, source snapshot, and relevant review evidence in the release record. Use the [completed verification receipts](../verification/) to establish the actual release status; a pending integration must not inherit credit from an earlier receipt.

These improvements have explicit stopping conditions. Once the central claims are correct, their evidence can be followed and reproduced at the stated level, and the author's intellectual contribution is visible, the thesis can make a strong case for excellence. New architectures, a full expensive rerun, complete Lean coverage, a human-subject study, or demonstrated downstream intervention gains are optional extensions unless the thesis chooses claims that require them.

## Review scope

The reviews concern the TeX and repository artifacts inspected on 9 November 2026 after the handover's main editorial corrections. The routing-law and regularizer-assumption repairs and the clarified publication framing are credited, not listed as outstanding defects. Several strongest findings were independently checked against tables, code, and saved scores. The suite is a critical source and artifact assessment with the executed checks described above; it is not an exhaustive theorem audit or a fresh full experimental reproduction. The detailed reviews retain the evidence and limits needed to evaluate each recommendation.
