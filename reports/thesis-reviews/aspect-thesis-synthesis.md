# Review of thesis synthesis and scholarly judgment

This review assesses the thesis as an integrated M.Sc. investigation: whether its research questions, theoretical contributions, experimental evidence, and conclusions form a coherent scientific argument. It applies the [assessment framework](assessment-criteria.md), with particular attention to the introduction, chapter organization, experimental framing, conclusion, and publication-provenance appendix. It does not substitute for the separate mathematical and experimental audits.

The research substance is strong enough to make a 1.0 assessment credible. The difficulty is not a shortage of material. It is that the manuscript asks the examiner to reconstruct too much of the hierarchy, provenance, and evidential status of that material. The shared conceptual argument is real, but the presentation does not yet make the strongest case for it. I would prioritize a focused synthesis revision over another major theoretical extension or benchmark campaign.

## Substantial strengths

The thesis identifies a meaningful relationship between its two constituent works. The first changes the representation available to attribution; the second makes the backward propagation rule an explicit mathematical object. The explanation that DC and stopping decompositions use the same sign split while retaining different information is particularly effective. It establishes a reason for the papers to belong together rather than merely sharing an application area. The experimental chapter explicitly returns to these two design choices. These are substantive achievements of synthesis. See [Introduction, lines 13–21](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex#L13) and [Experiments, line 3](../../tex/chapters/08_experiments.tex#L3).

The publication basis is now clear: one first-authored, coauthored **CVPR 2026 Highlight paper**, *Hidden Monotonicity*, and one first-authored, coauthored **preprint**, *Playing the Network Backward*. The introduction identifies the different statuses without implying AISTATS acceptance. A Highlight publication is substantial evidence of research achievement at master's level; the preprint must be judged on its arguments and evidence, not discounted merely because its publication process is incomplete. Neither status automatically validates the thesis's later extensions. See [Publication basis](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex#L23) and [Publication provenance](../../tex/chapters/10_appendix.tex#L805).

The manuscript also shows scientific judgment in separating quantities that would be convenient but misleading to combine. It explains the different Pixel Flipping definitions, perturbation budgets, sensitivity protocols, and ResNet architectures of the two experimental campaigns. It acknowledges that localization is not faithfulness, that no method dominates every metric, and that downstream model improvement remains untested. Its account of partial Lean verification distinguishes checked mathematics from Python correctness and experimental validity. These qualifications strengthen the thesis. They should remain prominent rather than disappear during polishing. See [Experimental setup](../../tex/chapters/08_experiments.tex#L5), [Limitations](../../tex/chapters/09_conclusion.tex#L19), and [Formal verification](../../tex/chapters/10_appendix.tex#L731).

## Five revisions that would materially strengthen the thesis

### 1. Close the research questions explicitly

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The three questions in [Introduction, line 21](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex#L21) are useful, but the conclusion answers them indirectly through a chapter-by-chapter recap. Six substantial contribution paragraphs then compete for equal attention. The reader must decide which theorem, experiment, or qualification actually answers each question.

The third question is the clearest example. It asks how the calculations can become useful for model improvement, while [Introduction, line 19](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex#L19) and [Conclusion, lines 29 and 71](../../tex/chapters/09_conclusion.tex#L29) correctly state that downstream correction, training, or steering has not been evaluated. This is an openly acknowledged boundary, not evidence of misrepresentation. Nevertheless, the question promises a broader answer than the empirical work supplies.

**Action:** Either formulate the third question around the properties needed for reliable attribution-based intervention, or conclude directly that the thesis establishes mathematical and empirical prerequisites rather than demonstrated downstream gains. Add a compact research-question table identifying the answer, decisive theorem or table, and remaining limitation. **Done when:** each question has an explicit closing answer with named evidence; no new intervention study is needed if the question and conclusion are scoped accordingly.

### 2. Make the contribution hierarchy and origins auditable

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The contribution list at [Introduction, lines 37–54](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex#L37) covers decomposition theory, stabilization, games, attribution methods, tropical geometry, and transformers. The publication paragraphs explain the overall reuse, but do not identify which specific results belong to which paper, which were corrected, and which constitute thesis-specific synthesis or extensions. First authorship also does not specify the student's conceptual, proof, implementation, or experimental roles.

This is a documentation gap about independence and intellectual organization, not a suggestion that collaborative research is unsuitable for a master's thesis. Coauthored research can fully support an excellent assessment. The examiner nevertheless needs enough information to assess the author's contribution without inferring it from author order or the quantity of technical material.

**Action:** Add a concise contribution-and-provenance table, preferably adjacent to the existing contribution list, with columns for result family, originating work, thesis addition or correction, and the author's actual role. Identify two or three central contributions and treat the remaining material as supporting developments. Roles must be supplied or confirmed by the author rather than inferred. **Done when:** an examiner can trace each headline contribution to its source and the student's work without consulting repository history.

### 3. Replace the blanket description of the transformer chapter as less rigorous

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The transformer extension is a named contribution and the setting for the principal localization result. Yet [the chapter's opening](../../tex/chapters/07_transformers.tex#L3) describes it as an outlook with “lighter rigor.” That is an unhelpfully broad classification of a chapter containing formal identities, conditional interpretations, implementation choices, and measured results. It risks weakening sound conditional statements while leaving the exact limits of other claims unclear.

The more precise explanation already exists in [Conclusion, line 27](../../tex/chapters/09_conclusion.tex#L27): the construction uses realized attention patterns and detached references, represents a specified Value-path backward rule, and does not recover the full unconditioned Query/Key derivative. This distinction should frame the contribution where it is first presented.

**Action:** Replace the blanket rigor label with a short map of established statements, conditioning assumptions, implemented rules, and remaining conjectural extensions. Carry the essential scope into the introductory contribution paragraph. **Done when:** the reader can identify exactly what the transformer mathematics establishes and what the experiments evaluate, without interpreting “outlook” as a substitute for hypotheses. Completing every possible attention-game formalization is not required for this repair.

### 4. Give the examiner a clear route through the technical depth

**Optional strengthening, with substantial presentational value.** The manuscript's breadth is impressive, but its organization sometimes obscures the boundary between preparation and contribution. The “Mathematical Foundations” chapter contains Imaginative Polytopes and the smooth splitting development that the introduction promotes as contributions. The game chapter first presents the games and modes, then introduces a separate notation section and develops the stopping and routing constructions in detail. A preview followed by proofs is legitimate; the reading hierarchy needs to be unmistakable. See [Foundations](../../tex/chapters/02_foundations.tex#L1), [Game framework](../../tex/chapters/04_game_theory.tex#L1), and [Notation and conventions](../../tex/chapters/04_game_theory.tex#L276).

**Action:** Mark established background, paper-derived results, and thesis extensions at the relevant section openings. Give a short reading route through the central definitions, results, and experiments, identifying which detailed proofs may be deferred on a first reading. **Done when:** a technically competent examiner can follow the thesis's main argument without treating every formal section as equally essential. Preserve the proofs and source content; shortening the thesis indiscriminately is not the objective.

### 5. Add an empirical synthesis that respects the separate campaigns

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The experiments are responsibly presented as two campaigns, with incompatible metrics explicitly distinguished. However, the combined thesis should make its comparative scientific lesson more explicit than “different methods excel at different criteria.” Relevant evidence is distributed between the main experimental chapter, the DCINN discussion in the methods chapter, the trajectory experiments, and the extended results. The examiner must assemble what each supports about the shared framework. See [Experiments](../../tex/chapters/08_experiments.tex#L1), [DCINNs](../../tex/chapters/05_saliency.tex#L245), and [Trajectory experiments](../../tex/chapters/11_trajectory.tex#L822).

**Action:** Add a short synthesis at the end of the experimental discussion: for each central objective, state which construction was tested, the observed benefit and cost, and what remains unestablished. Include the distinction between regenerating tables from summaries and rerunning evaluations. Do not manufacture a combined numerical ranking from incompatible protocols. **Done when:** the thesis provides a defensible account of when its structural choices help, when they trade one criterion for another, and where evidence is illustrative or unavailable.

## Overall examination judgment

This is an ambitious research thesis with an authentic unifying idea and unusually substantial theoretical and empirical material. It should not be asked to become a third paper or a complete formal verification programme to justify excellence. The strongest remaining master's-level requirement is disciplined scientific ownership: identify the central contribution, show its provenance, answer the questions with the evidence actually obtained, and make the limits visible at the point of claim. With that focused revision, the case for an excellent assessment would be materially stronger without expanding the research scope.
