# Review of Authorship Publication Provenance and Independent Contribution

The thesis has an unusually strong research basis for a master's project: a CVPR 2026 Highlight paper and a substantial preprint, both with the student as first author. The present introduction identifies those facts clearly. The remaining weakness is more specific: the manuscript does not yet give an examiner a sufficiently concrete account of the student's own contributions, the origins of individual results, and the tools used in preparing the thesis. Those are repairable documentation gaps, but they matter directly to assessing independent scientific work. Publication prestige cannot supply the missing account.

This review applies the [master's assessment framework](assessment-criteria.md) to the current TeX sources, publication author lists, declaration, bibliography, and correction records. It assesses attribution and provenance, not the correctness of every theorem or the execution of the experiments. It does not infer misconduct or assign an examination grade.

## What the thesis already does well

The [publication basis in the introduction](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex), lines 23–29, now distinguishes **Hidden Monotonicity**, published at CVPR 2026 and selected as a Highlight, from **Playing the Network Backward**, the preprint arXiv:2605.06212v2. It names the coauthors rather than presenting either collaboration as sole-authored work. The [provenance appendix](../../tex/chapters/10_appendix.tex), line 807, explicitly avoids claiming AISTATS acceptance. This is an appropriate and academically honest distinction; the preprint's status is not itself a weakness in a master's thesis.

The bibliography supports that account: `hiddenMono` records the CVPR proceedings and pages 24107–24117, while `playingBackward2026` identifies the versioned preprint. The official [CVPR listing](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2026/poster/36564) and [arXiv version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06212v2) provide stable external checks. The thesis also gives a substantive reason for integrating the works: the research questions and shared decomposition framework connect representation, backward semantics, method design, and evaluation. A third publishable project is not needed to justify that synthesis.

The provenance appendix makes a valuable distinction between the original game paper's AI-use statement and subsequent AI-assisted Lean development and thesis integration, at lines 809–825. The formalization discussion also acknowledges corrections and limits: lines 788–800 identify several mathematical repairs and distinguish proof checking from empirical certification. That candor should be retained.

## The author's individual contribution is not sufficiently identified

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The six contributions in the [introduction](../../tex/chapters/01_introduction.tex), lines 36–54, describe what the thesis contains. They do not establish which ideas, proofs, implementations, experimental decisions, and analyses the student personally developed within the two collaborations. The first-author statement and acknowledgements are useful context, but neither answers those questions. For example, the [acknowledgements](../../tex/frontmatter/acknowledgements.tex), lines 10–22, name discussions concerning DC decomposition, evaluation, parameter randomization, and trajectory measures; they intentionally do not allocate responsibility for the resulting work.

This is a gap in evidence of independence, not evidence that independence was absent. An examiner should not have to reconstruct intellectual ownership from Git authorship, author order, or an oral impression. It is particularly consequential where two collaborative research programmes supply most of the technical material.

Add a short, factual contribution statement covering each paper and the thesis integration. State the student's roles in conceptual development, mathematical proofs, software, experimental design, experiment execution, analysis, and writing, together with the principal complementary roles of collaborators. Use concrete tasks rather than unsupported percentages or a generic claim to have done everything. The author must supply and verify this information; it cannot be inferred by a reviewer or generated from the author list. Completion means an examiner can connect each central contribution to a documented student role without treating collaboration as a defect.

## Publication reuse and thesis additions need a result-level map

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The publication paragraph and appendix say that experimental settings and values are retained and that the thesis adds common definitions, connections, and Lean formalization. This is useful but too coarse for a long, reorganized manuscript. The heading “Contributions of This Thesis” can reasonably include earlier work incorporated into the thesis, yet the reader still needs to distinguish inherited results from extensions, rewritten exposition, and corrected claims.

Add a compact provenance table keyed to the six listed contributions and the principal experimental campaigns. Each row should identify the originating paper and version, the corresponding thesis sections or labels, and the nature of the thesis treatment: retained, reorganized, extended, or corrected. A table grouping related results is sufficient; marking every sentence would make the document less readable. Explicitly distinguish new empirical evidence from re-presented published results. Completion means a reader can determine where the thesis's added intellectual work lies without comparing two full source trees.

## The AI disclosure does not yet match the signed declaration

**Must correct before submission.** The [declaration](../../tex/frontmatter/declaration.tex), lines 9 and 26, asserts that product names, manufacturers, software versions, and purposes have been stated. The active thesis text names only generic “generative AI tools” in the quoted paper statement and “AI coding assistance” for subsequent work. A search of the TeX sources finds no corresponding product, manufacturer, or version inventory. This is a concrete internal documentation inconsistency, independent of any judgment about whether the assistance was appropriate.

Add a concise tool-use table, separating preparation of the original publications from later thesis editing, integration, formalization, and review. Record the actual products, providers, versions or model identifiers when retained, purposes, and affected artifacts. Where an identifier was not recorded or the service did not expose it, say so accurately instead of inventing precision. Explain the author's verification and adoption of assisted material. Preserve the historical scope of the quoted paper statement; do not silently extend its claim about theorem production to later Lean work. Completion means the disclosure and the declaration are factually consistent. The applicable declaration wording should be checked with the supervisor if an unavailable historical identifier prevents literal compliance.

## Corrections need a clear boundary between publication and thesis

**Needed for a persuasive 1.0 case.** The [correction record](../mathematical-corrections.md), lines 3–5, expressly says local changes do not update the published papers. Lines 99–108 also say that mathematical fixes alone do not establish that the experimental implementation and reported results are unaffected. The appendix appropriately mentions these repairs, but the provenance section's broad description of retained original protocols and values does not expose their relationship to the corrected theory.

Extend the provenance table with a brief correction column: original statement, thesis correction, affected result or method, and whether implementation impact has been checked. Refer to detailed evidence rather than reproducing the entire audit. This does not require a new experimental campaign by default; it requires an honest boundary around what the retained results establish. If impact remains unresolved, identify the specific question rather than implying either invalidity or immunity.

On this aspect, the thesis has the research substance to support an excellent assessment, but its documentation does not yet make the strongest independent-work case. The highest-value repairs are an accurate tool inventory, a factual contribution statement, and a concise map of publication reuse and corrections. More publication accolades or additional research would not substitute for them.
